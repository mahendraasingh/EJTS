How SmartSearch Works Under the Hood

1
Trie Prefix Autocomplete: Traverses character branches in O(L) time and extracts instant prefix suggestions as you type.
2
Inverted Index: Maps each unique word to list of matching document IDs for instant O(1) candidate lookup.
3
Context-Aware Scoring: Ranks results with exact title bonuses (+140), phrase matching (+85), and word proximity (+25).
4
Min-Heap Priority Queue: Retains only the top 10 most relevant documents in O(N log K) time.
