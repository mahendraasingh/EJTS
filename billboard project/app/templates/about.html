Smart Billboard AI Analytics

College Capstone / Production-Grade Edge Computer Vision Project

Smart Billboard AI is a self-contained edge application designed for high-density public digital signage. By leveraging a standard laptop's built-in webcam and optimized CPU-based neural inference, it quantifies audience engagement without requiring expensive GPU servers, cloud APIs, or proprietary sensors.

End-to-End Processing Pipeline

1
Webcam Video Capture (OpenCV):
Frames are captured in a dedicated background daemon thread via OpenCV VideoCapture at 640x480 resolution to prevent blocking HTTP endpoints.
2
Ultralytics YOLOv8 Neural Inference:
YOLOv8 nano (yolov8n.pt) processes frames targeting class 0 (Person) with configurable confidence threshold (default 0.45), running efficiently on standard multi-core laptop CPUs.
3
Centroid Proximity Tracking & Occlusion Handling:
Assigns temporary, persistent tracking IDs (e.g. #101) across consecutive video frames using Euclidean distance matching. Maintains state through a 2.5s occlusion tolerance window to prevent duplicate counts.
4
Central Monitoring Zone (ROI) Evaluation:
Evaluates person centroids against the calibrated central monitoring zone (default central 60% of frame) to isolate engaged viewers from distant background passersby.
5
Audience Session Lifecycle & Advertisement Attribution:
Upon entering the zone, a ViewerSession episode is recorded in SQLite and attributed dynamically to the currently visible billboard advertisement. When the person departs, duration is computed from exact timestamps.
6
Live Web Dashboards & Analytics Reporting:
Metrics are aggregated across selectable time windows (Today, 7D, 30D, All) and rendered using responsive Chart.js visualizations, with raw CSV export functionality.

Metric Definitions & Disclosures

Estimated Presence Duration:

The continuous duration in seconds a person remains inside the camera's monitoring zone. Academic note: Presence duration indicates proximity and orientation toward the display, not guaranteed eye gaze or cognitive attention.

Zone Occupancy vs. FOV Count:

FOV Count: All detected individuals in the camera's wide view. Zone Occupancy: Only individuals positioned within the calibrated audience interaction region.

Completed Sessions vs. Bounce:

Transitory glances shorter than 1.0 second are discarded to filter out noise, rapid strides, or detection flicker.

Audience Share %:

The percentage of total audience sessions captured by a specific campaign relative to all campaigns running in the selected timeframe.

Privacy-by-Design Architecture

  • No Facial Recognition: The system does not detect faces, compute face embeddings, or match identities against any database.
  • Ephemeral Tracking IDs: Temporary numerical identifiers (e.g. #42) exist solely in RAM while the individual is visible and expire automatically upon departure.
  • No Video Recording: Raw webcam frames are processed immediately in memory and discarded; no video files or user photographs are saved to disk.
  • 100% Local Inference: All neural weights run directly on the laptop CPU; zero images or telemetry are transmitted to external cloud servers.
